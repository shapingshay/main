About Shaping Shay

Shaping Shay is a project built around atmosphere, emotion, and texture. Each track explores layered instrumentation and cinematic space. Inspired by late‑night drives and quiet reflection, the project lives where sound and emotion intersect.

About Shay Herself

Shay is the feminine persona of an AMAB individual, unlocked during a gender identity journey. These songs are her creative outlet — telling stories of genuine thoughts, feelings, and experiences both before and after that awakening.

You can connect with Shay on Reddit: Shaping_Shay.
