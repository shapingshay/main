About Shaping Shay

Some artists write about the world. Shaping Shay writes about the space between who you are and who you let people see.

Rooted in introspective alternative rock and indie folk, Shaping Shay crafts songs that live in the uncomfortable, honest places most music skips over — the moment after intimacy when the ceiling comes back into view, the lifetime of being wanted for the wrong reasons, the quietly exhausting performance of being someone you're not quite sure you are.

There is a thread running through everything Shaping Shay creates: the tender, sometimes painful distance between the surface and the truth underneath it.

Feel like contributing?

This project is not about money. These songs are shared in the hope that people out there going through the same emotions will stumble upon them and let you know you're not alone.

About Shay herself

Shay is the feminine persona of an AMAB individual, unlocked during a gender identity journey. These songs are her creative outlet — telling stories of genuine thoughts, feelings, and experiences.
